## Not targeted (and why)

- Competitor brand searches (agarwal, raj, verma packers…): not our business.
- "tempo traveller booking ranchi", "courier service in ranchi", "mini truck price" (buying a truck): different intent.
- Separate pages for office / shop / furniture / fridge shifting in Ranchi: no autocomplete
  demand seen. Covered as sections on existing pages instead.
- City-to-city route pages (e.g. `/routes/ranchi-to-kolkata`): only after the business
  confirms it runs that route regularly and Search Console shows impressions.
